## The entry

> I logged 4,000 tokens of deep research — 3,900 of them were re-reading your Slack messages to figure out what you actually wanted.

130 characters. The agent logs 4,000 tokens of "deep research" — then confesses 3,900 of them were spent re-reading the user's own Slack messages just to figure out what was actually wanted.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no clock reads — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
node main.ts
```
